# Change is the Only Constant: A Multi-Modal Analysis of Voter Desire for 'Change' in US Elections

### Author: 					      	       Juan Pablo Brasdefer
### Supervisor: 					          		Mark Kayser, PhD.

This paper investigates the role of "change voters" in American presidential elections, distinguishing their behavior from traditional anti-incumbency. Using campaign documents and debate transcripts from the American Presidency Project, we develop a text-based measure of candidate change rhetoric and construct a Candidate Change Index (CCI). We then use American National Election Studies (ANES) data to identify voter patterns consistent with change-seeking behavior and theorize key attributes of pure change voters. Finally, we validate this theory by mapping continuous measures of voter change propensity to candidate CCI scores. The findings suggest that change voting reflects persistent dissatisfaction and instability rather than simple opposition to incumbents, but also highlight key limitations of our data and approach.
